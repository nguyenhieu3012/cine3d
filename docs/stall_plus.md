# Stall `plus` (21/11): a richer, livelier night street behind Tèo and Bíp, in our own engine

Opt-in: `setOpts: { plus: true }` (or `plus: { ...knobs }`) on the `stall` set. Off = pixel-identical (checked: 4 stills of the look test at 2 / 6 / 10 / 14 s are `inf dB` against the stills made before the edit; `python3 tools/regress.py` = **REGRESS PASS**, run twice, the second time at the very end).
An episode that uses it adds ONE line at the top so the detail maps are decoded before the set is built (main.js builds the set synchronously and sets filmReady without waiting for images):
`import '../packs/trada/stall_plus_preload.js';`   (without it the set still works, but the first frames of a headless render may have flat surfaces; a console warning says so).

## What is in `plus`
1. **Surfaces** (Poly Haven normal + roughness under the set's own colours, 1K JPG, repeat wrap, mipmaps, anisotropy 16, real-world tiling): wall plaster + dado `rough_concrete` at 1.4 m per repeat (normal strength 0.2, the wall colour x1.08 to give back the 7 % that maps + the removed light cone took, so the orange stays orange; measured R/G/B of a wall patch 110/60/24 -> 108/54/19), pavement `concrete_floor_worn_001` at 1.4 m (roughness 1.0 x map), wet asphalt `asphalt_02` at 1.6 m (roughness 0.4 x map, env 2 -> 0.6, no mirror). The colour maps are untouched (red tiles, green dado, orange wall).
2. **Things** (simple meshes in the set's drawn style; the engine has no GLB loader path, so no GLB): aluminium kettle with sooty belly, curved spout, lid knob, bail handle (replaces the small dark one, same stove); two flowered thermoses (replace the plain cylinders, same shelf places); potted plant with 18 curved leaves; roller shutter of 34 ribbed slats + rails + bottom bar + padlock (merged, 1 draw call); 8 more parked scooters on both kerbs (`makeBike`); 11 far-side building fronts and 9 near-side fronts along the stall's own building (6 painted facade variants: shop front with roller shutter or lit shop + sign, two floors of windows lit / dark / curtains / plants / "tiger cage" grilles / washing, balconies, awnings, air conditioners, roof tanks, merged into one mesh); the big end-of-street front; lamp posts with a shade, a small glow, a plinth, a pool of light, plus 2 more posts (emissive only, no extra light); 6 soft puddles (glossy, no mirror); a wet-looking road (lamp / tail-light highlights come from roughness + the point lights).
3. **Life** (every one a pure function of t; verified: the visible state of the set after `update(t)` is bit-identical whatever order the times were asked in): kettle steam (12 puffs, 3.3 s loop, strength breathing slowly); 3 moths round the hanging bulb (always above y 1.9, wings flapping); the bulb, shade, cable and its light swing about the ceiling point (about 3 degrees); bunting flutters; the two string-light runs and their bulbs sway; plant leaves move; the two blade signs (PHỞ, CAFE) stutter; a TV in a far window changes colour; scooters and a car pass; a walker on the far kerb; two leaves drift down near a lamp; 78 dust specks twinkle round the lamps. The old light shaft (cone under the bulb) is hidden; fog density x0.3.

## Knobs (all optional, in `plus: {...}`)
`fog` 0.3 (x scene fog density; 1 = as before, 0 = none) · `wetRough` 0.4 (asphalt, lower = wetter) · `normal` 1 (all normal-map strengths) · `breeze` 1 (sway strength) · booleans, default true: `surfaces props life street facades lamps puddles traffic walker tv specks`. Hooks kept: `fx.abandon` and the `abandoned` option hide traffic + walker; `steadyLights` skips the sweeping vehicle light (that mode freezes every point light it finds).

## Weaknesses (honest)
- Vehicles and the walker are boxes and capsules: they read as scooters / a car / a person only at distance and in motion, and the walker is a flat dark silhouette. In the widest shot the car passes at the right edge at z -10..+2 (x 5.5, beside the street, not between the camera and the cast) and is big there for a second.
- Facade detail is painted at 98 px per metre: fine behind the cast, soft if a shot ever frames a far front closely. Balconies / air conditioners are plain boxes.
- The wet road is modest: gloss and warm patches under the lamps and tail lights, small dark puddles. Seen from the two-shot camera the road is mostly out of frame; in the wide and street shots it reads wet but not shiny (by design: no mirror).
- Wall and pavement normal maps are subtle by choice (the channel's colours and drawn look first); the wall has a faint stucco grain, the dado a hint of it.
- Moths are 3 cm flecks: they read as small flicks beside the bulb only when the bulb is near the frame; steam is faint in shots that do not frame the stove (it is at x -1.5, outside the two-shot).
- With `plus` the stall lamps do not follow `lampS / lampR / blackout / cold` for the new pieces (the new facades, halos, TV stay lit; the old pieces still do); `steadyLights` freezes every point light, so the vehicle light is skipped there. Use `plus` for plain night episodes.
- The engine itself is not frame-order independent to the last pixel (a fresh page vs a page that rendered earlier times differs by ~38 dB in the OLD set too: some player state). Everything in `plus` is: its visible state after `update(t)` is identical whatever was asked before.
- Needs the one-line preload import in the episode, or the first frames can be flat.

## Round 2 (21/11): real vehicles and walker, lamp rules, louder moths and steam, automatic preload
Files: `packs/trada/stall_plus*.js` (new `stall_plus_veh.js`), one word added to the `buildStallPlus({...})` call line in `stall.js` (`cold,`). Look-test helpers: `out/stall_plus/r2/vehtest.js` (`--q "cam=street|wide|clip|sc|car|walk|sa"`, holds one camera for the whole 34 s cycle), `out/stall_plus/r2/fxtest.js` (`--q "fx=blackout=1"`, `cold=1`, `lampS=1,lampR=1&steady=1`). Deliverables in `out/stall_plus/r2/`: `sheet_wide_14.00.jpg`, `sheet_street_10.00.jpg`, `sheet_two_2.00.jpg`, `sheet_close_6.00.jpg` (before | after), `after_vehicles_closeups.jpg` (scooter B with passenger, car, walker x2, scooter A), `lamp_rules_normal_blackout_cold_steady.jpg`, `stall_plus_clip.mp4` (6 s, 60 fps), `before/`, `after/`.
1. **Shapes.** Car: dark rounded saloon (bevelled side profile pulled out sideways), glass cabin, roof, mirrors, 4 wheels with hubs, two glowing headlights, two red tail lights, plate, soft halos. Scooters: rounded body, leg shield, floor, seat, mudguard, fork + bars, headlight / tail light, helmeted rider leaning forward with arms to the bars and bent legs; scooter B carries a passenger. Walker: conical hat, head, torso, hinged hips / knees / shoulders / elbows, shoes, gait at 5 rad/s (knee bends on the forward swing, arms against legs); he now turns TOWARDS the doorway (before he turned away). Lights are MeshBasic (the vehicle's own battery, the stall's blackout does not kill them). Lanes moved to the far half of the street: car x 8.7, scooter B 9.0, A 9.6, C 9.7 (far kerb x 10.7, parked scooters 10.3; the walker stays on the kerb at 10.95). In the wide shot (camera x 0.4) the car is now a small dark shape far down the street; no vehicle is ever near the camera in the four shots or in the 34 s street / wide holds (checked at 12 times each).
2. **Lamp rules.** Cause found: the facade textures had lit windows, shop interiors and sign letters painted bright in the DIFFUSE map, so a blackout left them glowing. Now the diffuse is near black there and the glow is only in the emissive map (the stall's blackout / steadyLights code already scales every emissive material, including these). New in `lightCtl()` (stall_plus_lib.js; the stall's own formulas): facades get a cooler emissive colour and a blue-grey wash under `cold` / `blackout`; lamp halos, lamp-pool discs, the TV pane and the dust specks dim by blackout flutter, the cold unsteady lamp, and (steadyLights) lampR to 30 %; the vehicle point light is a child of the scene (not of the stall group) so the stall's cold / steady code no longer freezes it at its first value. Under `steadyLights`: no neon stutter, one steady TV colour, no twinkle, bulb + string lights hold still (bunting, plant, steam still move), no sweeping vehicle light or moving road pools (the vehicles and their own lights still pass). Moths glow through emissive, so blackout / lampR fade them with the bulb. `cold` option is passed from `stall.js`.
   Not changed: facades follow only lampR under steadyLights (like the old street group: lampS = string lights and signs of the stall itself); a faint amber residue of the windows remains in a full blackout (emissive 4 %).
3. **Moths / steam.** Four moths (two tight round the bulb, two wandering over the table at y 1.95-2.3), wings 4.2 x 3 cm, cream, glowing (emissive 1.0), flap 30 Hz. First try (5.5 cm, 1.5) looked like paper scraps next to the sign: shrunk. Steam: 14 puffs, 1.5 m tall, 0.24 m wide, opacity 0.95, drifts INTO the frame (it used to drift out of the left edge); visible as a pale plume at the left edge of the two-shot and the wide shot.
4. **Preload automatic.** `stall_plus.js` now does `import './stall_plus_preload.js'` (top-level await), so any module that imports the stall set waits for the six maps before the set is built; verified with an episode that does not import the preload (`out/stall_plus/r2/nopre.js`: no warning, all six images present). The manual import line is no longer needed (harmless if kept). Side effect: every episode that loads stall.js now fetches the 4 MB of JPG even with `plus` off (no pixel change; regress PASS).
Cost: unchanged order (clip renders at 36 fps, 2 workers).
