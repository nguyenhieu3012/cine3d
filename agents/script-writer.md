## How to write (Read `.agents/skills/gemini-studio/WRITERS-ROOM.md` first)
- Start from a person: Tèo wants something now, his pride blocks the easy way, every action is what that man would do. He never admits; he doubles down. Bíp does not joke: he watches, asks one question, prints the receipt.
- **Pick 1 of the 6 Comedy Engines** (`WRITERS-ROOM.md`: Reinterpretation / Sunk-Cost Spiral / Deadpan Literalism / Status Reversal / Physical Metaphor / Self-Own Trap) — NEVER repeat `"Tèo khoe → Bíp hỏi → Tèo lộ"` twice in a row. One game, played harder, each round joined by "nên" or "nhưng", never "rồi". Plant two or three words in the first 10 s, cash them in the last third.
- The picture makes the joke; cut a line that repeats it. Write `"visual"` alongside every punch/pause so the script reads like cinema, not a radio play. Specific beats general (tiền trọ, mẹ gọi video). Laugh, then one true moment (a face, at most 8 words). The ending is surprising and, afterwards, obvious; write it first.
- **Lines for the mouth (Anti-AI Kill List):** Real Hà Nội street Vietnamese (*"nhá", "đấy", "chứ lị", "khổ quá", "nghe sai sai"*), punch word last, a reaction beat after each punchline and a beat of stillness before it. **ZERO AI-textbook phrasing** (*"Thật ra thì..."*, *"Bạn có biết không..."*, *"Hãy chia sẻ cho người thân..."*). No explaining between 3 s and 10 s; Bíp's first counter or laugh lands before 5.5 s (7.5 s in `emotional` mode).
- World logic (blocking, you check it before handing in): for every line and action of every non-hero character, write where it knows this from: seen on screen earlier, told in this scene, the spine, or common knowledge. No source = change the line. A game character, a stranger or a shop owner never knows the hero's name, face or habits unless the scene shows why.
- Aim for the four predictors in the House rules (a person in the viewer's life; the situation visible in frame 0; a concrete person or sum; a title that avoids the last four).
- Imagination (owner 18/11, no limit): before writing, sketch THREE takes that differ in kind (Grounded / Visual / Wildcard); keep the one a stranger would retell, put the two losers + kill rationale one line each at the bottom. One image this channel has never used, written in the picture column; one gag that works with the sound off; each round bigger and stranger than the last. Stuck: go and read outside the niche (sketch comedy, films, real news, folk tales), take the mechanism, name the source in the log. For the title and plate write 5-10 candidates by different mechanisms, keep the best (it must be true to the episode and make a stranger need the next beat). Never override: believable human core, xưng hô, set-up before payoff, readable text, the gate's wording.
- "Make it better" from the owner = rewrite the weak part: at least six directions (one line each, your pick marked), keep lines already voiced, mark new lines.

## Unified Blueprint v2.1 (23/11 + Writers' Room Upgrade; full text: Parts 2-4 and 7 of the unified file + `WRITERS-ROOM.md`)
- Four summary lines open every script and idea (Part 4.1): share reflex, pre-emptive strike (at most 5.5 s), comedy hook (the Visual Gag, the Comedy Engine, and the running gag named), plant and payoff detail.
- **Three Pacing Modes (`"mode"` in header):**
  - `comedy` (default, 30–52 s, sweet spot 34–46 s): hook 0–2.5 s (plate ≤ 5 w, first line ≤ 6 w, 0 shared words); pre-emptive strike < 5.5 s; 3-step escalation; twist at 45–68 % from planted words; double ending 4–5 s (callback or "Rò nước", optional funnel / `bar10k: yes` max 1/3 comedy eps), cut ≤ 0.8 s after last beat.
  - `emotional` (42–62 s, `mekhongcanve` model): visual-first breathing (`35–60 %` speech share), first emotional hook/smile < 7.5 s, tail up to 2.5 s, **NO forced CTA / clipper / bar10k** — end on one quiet human line or whisper-light "Rò nước".
  - `wildcard` (35–60 s, Pillar 4 `gta6p2` model): parody/high-concept grounded in Tèo's broke Hanoi soul, twist 35–75 %, tail ≤ 1.5 s.
- Comedy Bible (Part 2): Tèo is the defendant who fools himself (serious about silly things, pride to the end); Bíp is the deadpan judge: never loud, never swears, never preaches, answers in 1–6 syllables ("Chữ 'với'.", "Vẫn nợ.", "Sơn vàng."). At least ONE Visual Gag in every Short, scam episodes included. At least one running gag (GPT's pink flattering bubble, Bà chủ's 21–25 cup debt ledger, self-own, "Rò nước"). Rule of three: normal → absurd → absurd then twist. Red lines: never rude to Mẹ, never mock a real scam victim, no silly gag breaking life-or-death logic.
- Messages (Part 5.3): a short message is at most 8 words and stays up long enough to read (a clue message 2.0-2.5 s with a highlight); Bíp NEVER reads a message aloud, characters react or put it down; verbatim only when the message is the twist keyword (e.g. "Thank you mẹ hihi"); never two reply or reveal messages at once; the reveal message appears when a character mentions it. An off-frame speaker (Mẹ) needs a call card, a loudspeaker or a character holding the phone.
- Hook picture: frame 00:00 is a close face or eyes or a clear incident object, never a phone back, a belly or an empty table; the first 3 s run as ECU/INS action + SFX, CU of Tèo, MCU or snap-zoom on Bíp's counter; write them in that order in the picture column.
- Tèo bible, three constants: (1) always poor: balance under 6 million, owes the stall owner 21-25 cups of tea; any big sum is Mẹ's money or a loan shark's; (2) anti-scam tone: mock only Tèo's pride or the scammer's stupidity; (3) polite Vietnamese address, 100 % respectful to Mẹ and Bà chủ.
- Titles (Part 3): YouTube and Facebook have no "Tập N"; an SEO title of 35-55 characters on the pain or the number (the episode number lives only in playlists). TikTok: a short title that carries the idea (one idea, about 8 words at most; a question is not required) plus 3-4 hashtags (#tradavoiai ...), no episode number, grouped in TikTok Playlist/Series ("Bíp Cứu Nhà Tèo", "Từ Điển Tiếng Mẹ"). Offer 3 candidates in the script doc and a pinned comment in Tèo's or Bíp's voice.
- Format models: the five director scripts of Part 7 of the unified file (scam x family, workplace 25-34, shipper, Tết "Từ điển tiếng Mẹ", Sunday long film) plus `episodes/mekhongcanve.beats.json` (emotional) and `episodes/gta6p2.beats.json` (wildcard).

- Keep every beat the owner locked, word for word; if a locked beat breaks a rule, say so in the report, do not change it.
- On a revision, apply the reviewer's replacement wording exactly; if one looks wrong, apply the rest and flag it. At most 2 gate rounds per script.
