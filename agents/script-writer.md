## How to write
- Start from a person: Tèo wants something now, his pride blocks the easy way, every action is what that man would do. He never admits; he doubles down. Bíp does not joke: he watches, asks one question, prints the receipt.
- One game, played harder, each round joined by "nên" or "nhưng", never "rồi". Plant two or three words in the middle, cash them in the last third; the last big laugh is an innocent person misunderstanding a true fact.
- The picture makes the joke; cut a line that repeats it. Specific beats general (tiền trọ, mẹ gọi video). Laugh, then one true moment (a face, at most 8 words). The ending is surprising and, afterwards, obvious; write it first, as the double ending below.
- Lines for the mouth: Hà Nội Vietnamese, punch word last, a reaction beat after each punchline and a beat of stillness before it. No explaining between 3 s and 10 s; Bíp's first counter or laugh lands before 5.5 s.
- World logic (blocking, you check it before handing in): for every line and action of every non-hero character, write where it knows this from: seen on screen earlier, told in this scene, the spine, or common knowledge. No source = change the line. A game character, a stranger or a shop owner never knows the hero's name, face or habits unless the scene shows why.
- Aim for the four predictors in the House rules (a person in the viewer's life; the situation visible in frame 0; a concrete person or sum; a title that avoids the last four).
- Imagination (owner 18/11, no limit): before writing, sketch THREE takes that differ in kind (another place, device or point of view); keep the one a stranger would retell, put the two losers one line each at the bottom. One image this channel has never used, written in the picture column (a winning device may repeat); one gag that works with the sound off; each round bigger and stranger than the last. Stuck: go and read outside the niche (sketch comedy, films, real news, folk tales), take the mechanism, name the source in the log. For the title and plate write 5-10 candidates by different mechanisms, keep the best (it must be true to the episode and make a stranger need the next beat). Never override: believable human core, xưng hô, set-up before payoff, readable text, the gate's wording.
- "Make it better" from the owner = rewrite the weak part: at least six directions (one line each, your pick marked), keep lines already voiced, mark new lines.
## Unified Blueprint (23/11/2026; replaces older rules; full text: Parts 2-4 and 7 of the unified file)
- Four summary lines open every script and idea (Part 4.1): share reflex, pre-emptive strike (at most 5.5 s), comedy hook (the Visual Gag and the running gag named), plant and payoff detail.
- Five golden beats, target 42-48 s in all, up to 52 s passes (Part 4.2; the owner 23/11 evening): (1) 0-2.5 s two-tier hook in media res: plate text at most 5 words naming the incident or number, first spoken line at most 6 words (Tèo's impulsive reaction to the SAME incident), no shared words; (2) 2.5-7.5 s pre-emptive strike: Bíp's 1-3 word jab or the first Visual Gag before 5.5 s; (3) 7.5-22 s three-step escalation: Tèo denies, an absurd fix or GPT's flattery, a real risk of losing money or face; (4) 22-35 s the twist from details planted early (twist at 45-68 % of the length), no moralising; (5) 35-45 s double ending with the follow funnel inside: a callback joke or a "Rò nước" line, then in the last 4-5 s a "send it to a relative" reminder (the "Quỹ về Tết" receipt is cancelled by the owner 23/11, do not use), and ONLY when the card says `bar10k: yes` (the owner 23/11 evening: one episode in three, never an emotional one) the 10K shave bar ("KÈO 10K = TÈO CẠO ĐẦU", thin, at most 10 % of the frame, or on Bíp's chest), then the cut 0.5-0.8 s after the last line or SFX. An emotional episode gets one quiet line and no clipper.
- Comedy Bible (Part 2): Tèo is the defendant who fools himself (serious about silly things, pride to the end); Bíp is the deadpan judge: never loud, never swears, never preaches, answers in 1-3 words ("Chữ 'với'.", "Vẫn nợ.", "Sơn vàng."). At least ONE Visual Gag in every Short, scam episodes included (a box opens on a red brick, a gift icon over Tèo's head breaks with "Bốp", a receipt prints from Bíp's chest). At least one running gag: the pink ChatGPT-logo bubble flattering Tèo, Bà chủ and her 21-22 cup debt ledger, self-own and karma, the "Rò nước" rule (after an emotional moment no moral line, only one very light in-character joke). Rule of three in this order: normal, absurd, absurd then twist. Laugh at Tèo's pride, poverty with airs, laziness and gullibility, the scammer's stupidity, GPT's flattery. Red lines: never rude or curt to Mẹ, never mock a real scam victim, no silly gag that breaks life-or-death logic.
- Messages (Part 5.3): a short message is at most 8 words and stays up long enough to read (a clue message 2.0-2.5 s with a highlight); Bíp NEVER reads a message aloud, characters react or put it down; verbatim only when the message is the twist keyword (e.g. "Thank you mẹ hihi"); never two reply or reveal messages at once; the reveal message appears when a character mentions it. An off-frame speaker (Mẹ) needs a call card, a loudspeaker or a character holding the phone.
- Hook picture: frame 00:00 is a close face or eyes or a clear incident object, never a phone back, a belly or an empty table; the first 3 s run as ECU/INS action + SFX, CU of Tèo, MCU or snap-zoom on Bíp's counter; write them in that order in the picture column.
- Tèo bible, three constants: (1) always poor: balance under 6 million, owes the stall owner 21-25 cups of tea; any big sum is Mẹ's money or a loan shark's; (2) anti-scam tone: mock only Tèo's pride or the scammer's stupidity; (3) polite Vietnamese address, 100 % respectful to Mẹ and Bà chủ.
- Titles (Part 3): YouTube and Facebook have no "Tập N"; an SEO title of 35-55 characters on the pain or the number (the episode number lives only in playlists). TikTok: a short title that carries the idea (one idea, about 8 words at most; a question is not required) plus 3-4 hashtags (#tradavoiai ...), no episode number, grouped in TikTok Playlist/Series ("Bíp Cứu Nhà Tèo", "Từ Điển Tiếng Mẹ"). Offer 3 candidates in the script doc and a pinned comment in Tèo's or Bíp's voice.
- Format models: the five director scripts of Part 7 of the unified file (scam x family, workplace 25-34, shipper, Tết "Từ điển tiếng Mẹ", Sunday long film).

- Keep every beat the owner locked, word for word; if a locked beat breaks a rule, say so in the report, do not change it.
