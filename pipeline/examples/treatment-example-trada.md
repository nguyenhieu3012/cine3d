# Treatment: ngay12v4 (Tập 58, "Tèo nhịn hạt", Ngày 12) - director's treatment, written 13/11 after the fact

Copy of `ngay12` for the v4 pipeline trial. The owner's fixed points (law, his words 13/11): "Tập này làm rất ok, từ kịch bản đến nội dung" (every spoken line, the beat order and the story are frozen); "Mở đầu hiện chữ no nut november lên cho khán giả hiểu" (the hook plate); "khung hình bị giật, k mượt" (smoothness is a fault); "Logo làm nổi bật lên nhé" (Bíp's Claude spark stands out). Times below are the measured voice times (`episodes/ngay12v4.times.js`, 75.0 s).

## About
Twelve days into a challenge everyone recognises but nobody names, Tèo claims a reward: he can hear what people think. He uses it to win, first a glass of watered tea, then 500,000 đ a friend meant to dodge, until he turns it on Bíp, who has been reading something better, Tèo's browser history, and his mother calls about 2 a.m. The film is about this: hearing other people's hearts feels like power until someone reads yours, and the one who finally reads you is your mum.

## Feelings
1. Knowing amusement (0-10 s): we get the sealed tissue box before Bíp says a word; we are in on the joke.
2. Wish-fulfilment (10-34 s): the power pays, small then real money; we enjoy Tèo winning against two people we all know (the stall-owner who waters the tea, the friend who never pays back).
3. Dread turning to squirm (34-75 s): the tables turn on Tèo, first by Bíp, then by Mum, and the laugh is at him, ending in a cringe that still feels warm (Mum is happy).

## Cause chain
- Because Tèo has held out twelve days (sealed box, dark circles, trembling hand), Bíp asks what he gets for it; therefore Tèo shows the reward: he hears thoughts (9.98 s).
- Because he hears Bà's real thought ("yesterday's ice, topped up with water"), he quietly pushes the glass to Bíp's side (18-20 s).
- Because he hears Hùng's thought ("tomorrow I change my number"), he repeats it out loud; therefore a scared Hùng pays the 500,000 đ on the spot (+500.000đ card, 29 s).
- Because the power has now paid real money, Tèo grows cocky and turns it on Bíp ("Giờ tới mày", 31 s); Bíp's cloud is empty, then fills: "2 a.m. last night, what were you searching?"
- Because the 2 a.m. secret is out, Tèo panics (hand on the face-down phone) and lies: "growing vegetables"; Bíp, who only says facts, adds "forty tabs".
- Because Tèo was online at 2 a.m. (planted by Bíp 11 s earlier), Mum calls about it; the phone that rings is the one his hand has been hiding.
- Because Tèo repeats his vegetable lie to Mum and she asks Bíp, Bíp repeats the fact ("Bốn mươi tab"); because Mum hears "Tab" as a girl's name, she is delighted and asks to meet Tab at Tết; therefore Tèo's own lie traps him, and Mum's thought finishes him.
- Because the box is still sealed, the challenge goes on to Day 13, whose power ("NHÌN THẤU") makes Bíp cover his chest.
- Weakest link (for the gate to judge): Mum's call comes at the exact moment; the cause is planted (online at 2 a.m.), the timing is coincidence. Kept: owner-approved.

## Tension curve
Per beat in `ngay12v4.beats.json`; per stretch, 0-10, no equal neighbours, a dip before every peak:
- 0-8 s hook and set-up: 5 -> 4 -> 4 -> 5 (Tèo trembling, Bíp needles him).
- 8.3-9.9 s wind-up: 3 (lights sink, music almost gone, slow push) -> 10.0 s the power: 8.
- 12-20 s round 1, the tea: drops to 2 (Bà smiling), thought 4, the glass push 3.
- 20-31 s round 2, the money: 4 -> 5 -> 7 (Tèo calls the dodge) -> 6 -> 5 -> 4 (paid, Bíp counts it).
- 31-48 s round 3, Bíp: 6 cocky -> 4 empty cloud -> 5 -> 4 "Có." (the dip) -> 7 the 2 a.m. thought -> 6 -> 8 the turn "Tao đọc lịch sử" -> 5 -> 4.
- 48-69 s Mum: 6 the ring -> 7 -> 5 the lie -> 6 -> 8 Bíp's "Bốn mươi tab" -> 5 "Tab là con nhà ai?" -> 7 -> 8 "Tết dẫn Tab về" -> 10 Mum's thought (the top, at 67-69 s, 89 % of the film).
- 69-75 s fold and tag: 4 -> 5.
Escalation by stakes, as the script planned: a glass of tea (a few thousand) -> 500,000 đ -> Tèo's dignity in front of his mother.

## 0-3 s
First frame: close on Tèo meditating, eyes shut, dark circles, his hand gripping the table edge, a tremble; the hook plate "NO NUT NOVEMBER / NGÀY 12: ĐƯỢC GÌ?" is already up; he says, calm, "Tao chưa đụng vào. Không một lần." At 0.95 s cut to the table: the "NGÀY 12" tally card and the tissue box taped shut "NIÊM PHONG 1/11" (the joke lands in the picture, nobody names it). 2.54 s Bíp, flat: "Đụng vào gì?" = first laugh.

## 4-10 s
3.5 s Tèo names it only as "Thử thách tháng này" (frozen line; it explains, which the v4 4-10 s rule dislikes; the gate approved it on 13/11 as the minimum a new viewer needs). 6.3 s Bíp says the hook plate's question out loud: "Nhịn mười hai ngày. Được gì?" - the challenge turns into a demonstration. 8.3-9.9 s nothing is said: the lights sink, the music nearly stops, the camera pushes slowly into Tèo's face. 9.98 s the eyes open gold, aura, dust, hair lifting: "Tao nghe được suy nghĩ." The answer to the plate arrives at 10 s; there is no explaining after it.

## Last 15 s
60.05 s Mum: "Tab là con nhà ai?" (the misreading) - Tèo freezes. 61.9 s he snaps back into the meditation pose of frame 1: "Mẹ... mẹ ơi, con đang thiền!" (the meditation planted at 0 s, cashed). 64.4 s Mum, suddenly happy: "Tết dẫn Tab về mẹ xem nhé." 67.0 s a thought cloud rises out of the phone: "Cuối cùng cũng có đứa chịu nó." - the top of the film, the laugh at Tèo's expense, warm because she means it. 69.1 s the fold: face into his palm, the aura dies; cut to the tissue box, still sealed (paid from 1 s); the unlock card "NGÀY 13: NHÌN THẤU ???" appears before anyone speaks, then Bíp reads it, covers his chest and looks into the lens. 73.0 s follow pill "THEO DÕI XEM NGÀY 13", 2.0 s tail, end at 75.0 s.

## Camera and colour grammar
- Real stall: warm evening light; Tèo keeps his gold rim, Bíp his cyan and the glowing Claude spark; the power is gold (eyes, aura, dust); every thought is a white cloud over the thinker, mouth closed, the cloud's words exactly the heard line and never doubled by a caption.
- The camera pushes in on whoever holds the power and pulls away from Tèo when he loses it (the pull at "Mày... đọc máy tao?"). Closest frames and punch-ins only at the three turns: the gold eyes (ECU, 10 s), "Tao đọc lịch sử" (low close on Bíp with a punch at "lịch sử", 43 s), the fold (69 s).
- Wides only to re-place people: Hùng is visible on his phone in the wide at 11.7 s before he speaks.

## Sequences (intentions)
- Hook (0-10 s): the audience knows what is not being touched; Tèo does not say it. Held back: what the reward is, until 10 s.
- Tea (12-20 s): we hear Bà's thought, Tèo hears it, Bà does not know; the payoff is silent (the glass slides to Bíp, Bíp looks at it, at him).
- Money (20-31 s): Hùng's smile and his thought contradict; Tèo says the thought aloud; the card "+500.000đ" appears while nobody speaks. The power is real.
- Bíp (31-48 s): the audience sees the empty cloud before Tèo understands; the cut lands on "lịch sử". Tèo's hand goes to the phone: plant for the ring.
- Mum (48-69 s): the phone under his hand rings on a silent beat with the "MẸ" call card; we know the truth, Mum does not, Bíp only says facts. Tèo begs with his hands; Bíp says "Bốn mươi tab" anyway.
- Tag (69-75 s): the sealed box and Day 13.

## Sound plan
- Voice always on top (>= 6.3 dB over music and effects in every line, per `voice.py build`); the old mix had 6 lines under that margin: rebuild with the new default build, no line re-timed, no take re-recorded.
- Music (ghost mood) follows the tension curve: thin under the tea, rising through the money and Bíp, fullest in the Mum stretch, a breath before the top; after the top (tender 10) it thins for the fold.
- Accents: riser into a bloom at the gold eyes (impact), slide + clink as the glass moves, ka-ching on +500.000đ, a thud on "lịch sử" (impact), phone buzz + ring in the 1.5 s silence before Mum, a pop on each thought cloud, lock + chime on the Day 13 card.
- Thoughts: the same voice, echoed and quieter; Mum through a phone speaker; room tone (street) under everything, never digital silence.
- Nobody has heard this mix: numbers only until the owner listens.
